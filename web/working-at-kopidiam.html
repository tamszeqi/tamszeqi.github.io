Working In Our Cafes

Be the face of KopiDiam. Our retail teams are passionate hosts who create a welcoming sanctuary for our guests, crafting perfect coffee and building community one cup at a time.

You'll receive comprehensive training in the art of coffee, with clear career paths to help you grow from a talented team member into a future leader within our cafes and beyond.

The Art of Roasting

Master the heat and the bean. Our roasters and production team are the guardians of quality, turning green beans into the perfect roast profile that defines KopiDiam.

Join a team dedicated to precision, science, and the sensory art of coffee. You will work with state-of-the-art equipment and ensure every bag of beans meets our gold standard.

Headquarters & Strategy

Drive the brand forward. From HR and Finance to our Creative Studio, our corporate team builds the infrastructure that allows our cafes to thrive.

We look for strategic thinkers and creative problem solvers who share our passion for people and coffee. Help us shape the future of KopiDiam.

Tech & Innovation

Innovating the future of coffee. Our tech team builds the digital experiences that connect our customers to their daily brew, from our mobile app to our loyalty platforms.

If you love coding, data analysis, or UX design, join us in creating seamless digital solutions that enhance the human connection of coffee.

Global Coffee Sourcing

Traveling the globe for the perfect cup. Our sourcing team builds relationships with farmers and ensures we are purchasing ethically and sustainably.

This role is for those with a deep understanding of agronomy and a passion for fair trade. You will be the bridge between the farm and the cafe.

Brand & Storytelling

Sharing our story with the world. Our marketing team crafts the campaigns, events, and stories that bring the KopiDiam brand to life.

From social media management to organizing community coffee tastings, you will be the voice that engages and excites our loyal customers.
