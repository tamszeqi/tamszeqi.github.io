worker welfare

Support for Worker Welfare

KopiDiam's ethical sourcing places people at the heart of every harvest. We partner with tea plantations that prioritise the well-being of their workers, ensuring that every hand behind our crops is treated with dignity and respect.

01.
Safe Working Conditions

Provide protective gear and safe environments to reduce injuries.

02.
Fair Wages

Pay workers fairly for their skills and effort.

03.
Community Support

Support local projects to improve community well-being.

04.
Healthcare & Education

Give workers and their families access to basic healthcare and learning opportunities.

05.
Proper Housing

Ensure clean, safe, and comfortable living spaces.

06.
Recognition & Incentives

Reward workers to motivate and appreciate their efforts.

ecosystem care

Biodiversity & Ecosystem Protection

We work with tea-growing communities to safeguard the natural environments that make our crops unique. This means preserving the natural forests surrounding tea plantations, promoting shade-grown cultivation that supports healthier soil and cooler microclimates, and actively protecting local flora and fauna. By maintaining these ecosystems, we ensure that every cup of KopiDiam tea or coffee reflects not just exceptional flavour, but a dedication to Malaysia's rich biodiversity and long-term environmental resilience.

Verifiable sustainability practices.
