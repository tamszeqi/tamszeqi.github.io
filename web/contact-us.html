Top 10 Most Popular FAQ's

We are experts in Malaysian flavours. Our team combines years of culinary experience with a deep passion for local heritage, ensuring every dish reflects authentic taste at a premium standard.

What kind of food do you serve?

We serve Malaysian-style dishes including Nasi Lemak, Mee Siam and other favourites.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

Yes! You can enjoy our dishes at home via GrabFood or Foodpanda, or call us directly for takeaway orders.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes! We offer meat-free versions of our signature dishes, including Vegetarian Curry Mee and Nasi Lemak (served without anchovies) upon request.

Do you cater for events?

Absolutely! We provide catering services for office gatherings and small parties. Please contact our manager for a custom menu quotation.

What are your opening hours?

We are open daily from 7:30 AM to 9:30 PM. Our signature breakfast sets are served until 11:00 AM.

Are your ingredients locally sourced?

Yes, we prioritize fresh, locally sourced ingredients to support Malaysian farmers and ensure the most authentic taste in every dish.

Are reservations needed?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for groups of 6 or more, we recommend making a reservation to secure your table.

Do you offer loyalty programs or promotions?

Yes, join our "Kopi Kaki" membership to earn points on every RM1 spent and redeem free drinks and exclusive meals!

Can I customize my order?

Of course! You can request "Kurang Manis" (less sugar) for drinks, or adjust the spice level for most of our dishes.
