More Stars, More Rewards

Rewards members can now collect Stars through purchases on Mobile Order & Pay, earn Stars as much as you can and redeem your available rewards with us. Not a member yet? Register now and begin your Star journey right away.

Now just a few steps away to get your favorite coffee

1. Log into the mobile app.

2. Tap on "Order" and explore the menu.

3. Choose for your favorite beverage.

4. Select a nearby pick-up store (within 3km radius).

5. Place your order, and pay ahead of time.

6. Receive a notification once your order is ready.

7. Pick-up and enjoy!

Terms and Conditions

  • Maximum purchase quantity is limited to 10 items, per order.
  • Multiple cards payment is currently unavailable.
  • Waiting times may vary depending on some unavoidable factors, such as peak hours.
  • Any ready for pick-up Order is advisable to collect no more than a reasonable period of fifteen (15) minutes, do bear the risk of deterioration in quality or change in condition of the item(s) if you fail to pick-up.
  • In the event of unreasonable delays in Pick-Up attributable, the Order shall not be entitled to a replacement or refund.
  • Orders can be placed up to 30 minutes before the store closes.
  • Ensure Notifications are allowed under Settings in the Starbucks MY app and mobile device to receive push notification when your Mobile Order is ready for pick up.
  • Mobile Order & Pay only applicable for any store located within 3km radius from your area.
