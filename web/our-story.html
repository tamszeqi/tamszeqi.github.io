Company Introduction

We aim to redefine the kopitiam culture in a contemporary setting, making it not only a place to eat, but a place to connect, share, and rediscover the taste of home.

ABOUT KOPIDIAM

Company Stories

Malaysia's greatest stories begin at the breakfast table — a cup of coffee, the aroma of toast, and the comfort of familiar flavors. Yet as life grows faster, those cherished kopitiam moments slowly fade into memory.
Kopidiam was created to bring them back — in a way that feels fresh and elevated. We honour the original recipes that shaped our culture, while refining the experience with premium ingredients and modern craftsmanship.
Every sip and every bite carries a taste of home — warm, nostalgic, and proudly Malaysian. Here, tradition isn't forgotten — it is celebrated, renewed, and shared.

ABOUT US

Dedicated to the Taste of Home

The Kopidiam team is built by passionate Malaysians who love our local flavors. Our chefs bring authentic skills from traditional kopitiams, combined with modern techniques to refine every dish. Our baristas craft each cup with care, keeping the true taste of Malaysia alive.Together, we work with one goal — to serve the flavors we grew up with.
